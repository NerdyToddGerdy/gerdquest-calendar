# Product principles

These are standing rules for GerdQuest Calendar. They apply to every change, by people
or by AI agents, and override convenience or growth ideas that conflict with them.

## 1. No microtransactions

GerdQuest Calendar will **not** become a microtransaction game.

- **Cosmetics are earned, never bought.** Skins, dice styles, calendar papers,
  page edges, avatar tokens, token borders, keepsakes and any future cosmetic
  category are unlocked only by playing (days played, milestones,
  achievements, choosing heroes). There is no store, currency, bundle or
  "premium" track for them.
- **No paid shortcuts or advantages.** Nothing may be bought that unlocks
  pages early, skips days, restores lost progress, adds rerolls, boosts
  stats, refills pools, or otherwise changes play.
- **No real-money currency.** In-game currencies (glint, gold, credits…) are
  earned in play only and can never be purchased or converted.
- **No pressure mechanics.** No loot boxes or randomised paid rewards, no
  timers or streaks designed to sell relief, no ads, no "limited time" offers.

## 2. The only potential revenue is campaigns

The one acceptable future monetary source is **the campaigns themselves**: a
player may buy a campaign (its year of pages) once, and then has all of it,
including everything earnable inside it (see issue #3). Buying a campaign
must not include or sell cosmetics, advantages or unlocks beyond the
campaign's own content; cosmetics tied to a campaign are still earned by
playing it.

## 3. Everyone can play for free

The bundled free campaigns stay free and complete, with every cosmetic and
achievement earnable without paying.

## How to apply

- Reject or rework any feature, issue or PR that would sell cosmetics,
  convenience or advantage, or that adds paid currency.
- When designing rewards, tie them to play (days played, milestones,
  achievements, story choices), never to payment.
- Privacy and terms pages must describe any future purchase as "buy a
  campaign" only.
