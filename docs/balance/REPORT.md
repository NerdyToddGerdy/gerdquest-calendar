# Balance report

Generated by the balance simulator (`npm run sim`). Each campaign is played 200 times with a deterministic policy bot (seeds 1000..1199), before and after the tuning in this change.

## How to run it

```sh
npm run sim                                  # all four campaigns, 200 runs each (about 2.5 minutes)
SIM_RUNS=50 SIM_CAMPAIGNS=halcyon npm run sim  # quick look at one campaign
SIM_OUT=/tmp/sim/out.json SIM_REPORT=/tmp/sim/report.md SIM_BASELINE=/tmp/sim/earlier.json npm run sim
```

`SIM_OUT` writes the raw summary JSON (plus per-run records beside it), `SIM_REPORT` writes the tables below, and `SIM_BASELINE` (an earlier `SIM_OUT`) adds a "before" table for each campaign. A light smoke test (`tests/sim-smoke.test.ts`) runs one seed per campaign in the normal `vitest run`.

The bot lives in `packages/engine/src/sim/` (pure TypeScript, no DOM): `policy.ts` is the player, `run.ts` plays a campaign and records events, `stats.ts` builds the tables.

## What the bot does

It picks options by expected outcome (trait plus modifiers against each check's band thresholds, effects valued in rough health points), rests when hurt or before fights, drinks healing below 40% health outside fights (heal abilities below 70%, also mid-fight), spends add-die, reroll, Boon, slot-die items and companions only when that raises the expected result of the pending roll, buys two or three healing items, better gear and cheap upgrades, equips the best gear, hires cheap help, takes hidden loot, plays the lottery, repairs and refits the ship, and fights dogfights with the same expected-value logic. Each run prefers a different hero at hero-choice pages (rotating through the choices) so every hero gets played.

Notes on reading the numbers:

- Dying is never run-ending in these rules: the hero returns at reduced health after a death-table roll. So "deaths" are death-table hits, and every run finishes the year (100% in all rows). The interesting figure is how often each month or boss page triggers the table.
- "% runs with a death" is the share of runs that hit the death table at least once that month. The final month is the "December bosses" figure (September for Starwake, which currently ends at month 9).
- The bot is competent but not perfect, and hero strength varies a lot (see "Deaths per hero"): Garrick and Brask are far sturdier than Pell, Ysolde, Marisol (Halcyon pilot) or Kit. Hero imbalance is a content problem this change does not try to fix.
- Death spirals matter: a death can cost gold, gear or a stat point, which raises the odds of the next death, so lethality responds steeply to small enemy changes.

## Before tuning (findings that motivated it)

- Ember Road, Halcyon and Starwake were brutal: a median of 10, 17 and 12 death-table hits per run, 75-95% of runs hitting the table in most months, and every boss page killing 45-95% of runs. Boss and elite toughness (8-12) sat out of reach of d6/d8 damage dice, so fights dragged on while enemies hit for 3-6 a round; hits values above 14 meant the hero's static Defense never blocked.
- Halcyon's prologue pages (the first six) were not the problem: no run reached 0 HP there with this bot. The harshness began with Captain Vell at the end of January (65% of runs died on that page) and never let up.
- Starwake's early game was the worst for new players: 14.5% of runs hit 0 HP in the first week and 52% in month 1; the Brine Foreman's Dredge killed 95% of runs in month 2.
- Brightwatch was the healthiest but front-loaded: 25% of runs died in month 1 and 40% in month 2 (Ink Eels, Reef Wreckers, Pale Herald), then December was a pushover (3%).

## What was tuned

Only numeric enemy fields changed (`ac`, `toughness`, `hits`, `dmg`) in the campaign enemy files; no story text, page structure, rewards or prices. Values ramp across the year (easier early, close to or above the old numbers for the last bosses), with extra relief for a few outlier fights.

| Campaign | Early months | Late months | Special cases |
|---|---|---|---|
| Brightwatch | toughness x0.85, damage x0.75, attack/defence thresholds -1 | toughness x1.25, damage x1.3, thresholds +1 | none |
| Ember Road | toughness x0.8, damage x0.7, thresholds -1 (bosses x0.75 / x0.6) | toughness x1.0, damage x1.0 (final bosses x1.15 / x1.2, thresholds +1) | Kennelmaster x0.65/x0.6, Hollow Warden x0.7/x0.6 |
| Halcyon | toughness x0.8, damage x0.7, thresholds -1 (bosses x0.75 / x0.6) | toughness x0.85, damage x0.9, hits -1 (bosses x0.85 / x0.95) | Bloom and Spore Swarm x0.6, Iolo Fane x0.65, Choir Sentinel x0.7 |
| Starwake | toughness x0.75, damage x0.65, thresholds -1 (bosses x0.7 / x0.55) | toughness x0.9, damage x0.95 (final bosses x0.95 to x1.0) | Tidewarden and Dredge x0.5-0.6, Teague x0.75, Roc Matriarch x0.65; Gilt Gull damage 6 -> 7, thresholds +1 |

(Values are rounded to whole numbers, floors of 2 toughness and 1 damage; see the diff for the exact enemy values.)

One existing test (`tests/classic.test.ts`, static Defense) asserted exact Frost Hound and Hush Cultist stats; it now reads those enemies from content, so the rule it checks (static Defense at or above the attack halves damage) is unchanged.

## Remaining issues

- Hero spread: in every campaign one or two heroes (Garrick, Brask, Wren, Bram) take under 0.5 deaths a run while others take 3-8. Fix at the hero level (abilities, HP, gear) rather than by enemies.
- Ember Road, Halcyon and Starwake economies are tight: median currency stays at 10-45. The bot buys healing and gear as it goes, so this is mostly spending, but a few more rewards or cheaper gear would help the weaker heroes.
- Ember Road and Starwake December/September bosses are slightly gentler than the 30-50% target.
- Death-table hits per run are also driven by classic's "lose all gold" entry; consider whether that is intended.
