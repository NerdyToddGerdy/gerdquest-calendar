Privacy

Version 1.0, 24 November 2026

Short version: GerdQuest Calendar has no accounts, no server of its own, no analytics and no cookies. Your progress stays on your device.

What is stored, and where

Your campaigns, hero, dice history, notes, progress record (days played, achievements, keepsakes, weekly chests and the look you chose for each campaign) and settings (such as light or dark mode) are saved in your browser's local storage on the device you play on. GerdQuest Calendar does not send them anywhere, and we cannot see them. Clearing site data in your browser, or using a different browser or device, means starting fresh unless you have exported a backup.

Export and import

"Export" builds a save code on your device: one line of text that you copy (or save as a text file). "Import" reads a code you paste, or a file you choose, also on your device. Neither sends anything over the internet.

What we do not do

No cookies, no tracking, no advertising, no analytics, no fingerprinting. Because there are no cookies or tracking, there is no cookie banner.

Third parties

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. When you load a page, GitHub receives your request and may log your IP address, as described in GitHub's privacy statement. We do not receive those logs.

Pages load two fonts, Cormorant Garamond and Inter, from Google Fonts. Your browser contacts Google to fetch them, which shares your IP address and browser details with Google under Google's privacy policy.

Children

We collect no personal information from anyone, so there is nothing to collect from children either.

Contact

Questions or requests: open an issue on GitHub.

Changes to this page

This policy describes GerdQuest Calendar as it works today. It will be updated before accounts, cloud saves, purchases or analytics are added, and the date and version above will change when it is.
