# The Lamp at Brightwatch — the year

Authored for 2024 (Jan 1 = Monday). Mon–Fri pages + one Sat/Sun weekend page,
314 pages through Dec 31. Weeks 1–7 (Jan 1 – Feb 18) are the hero trial and
the first expedition; this outline continues from the week-7 hook: *the
second bell's rope is fraying*.

**The arc — the Sevenfold Peal.** Long ago, seven bells rang together to
lull the Pale Tide (the fog's source, asleep beneath the reef) into its long
sleep. Five were lost. Brightwatch must find, recover, and re-hang all seven,
then ring them together on the longest night. Opposing them: the **Quiet
Choir**, a grey-robed order led by **Abbess Morrow Vane**, who believe the
fog's silence is mercy and want the Tide to wake.

Level-ups (the `levelUp` effect, on Monday "commendation" pages after a
month's boss): L3 Apr 1 · L4 May 13 · L5 Jun 24 · L6 Aug 5 · L7 Sep 16 · L8 Oct 28.

Cadence: weekend pages are Rest + market (+ the Sunday lottery from the
rules) unless marked **boss weekend**. Most month bosses fall on a Friday so
the weekend Rest follows.

| Weeks (Mondays) | Chapter | Highlights |
|---|---|---|
| Feb 19, 26 | **The New Rope** | The drowned lighthouse's chart of the Sevenfold Peal. Weave a new bell-rope: cliff-spider silk at Gullspire, kelp-hemp from the Saltmarket. Re-hang the second bell. Boss Fri Mar 1: *the Rope-Gnawer*. |
| Mar 4, 11, 18, 25 | **The Marsh Bell** (bell 3) | Hollowmere Fens, fen-folk, the Bog Choir. First sight of the Quiet Choir stealing clappers. Boss Fri Mar 29: *the Sedge-Mother*. Gear: Sedge Cloak. Companion: Bramble (toad). |
| Apr 1 – 29 | **The Mountain Bell** (bell 4) | L3. Gullspire climb, the High Tarn monks, the Choir took their bell; chase over the moor. Boss Fri Apr 26: *the Tarn Serpent*. Spring fair (Apr 29 week). Companion: Wick (firefly). |
| May 6 – 27 | **The Auction Bell** (bell 5) | Saltmarket: Madam Quill's Syndicate auctions a "curio bell". L4. The heist. Boss Fri May 24: *the Hushknight*. |
| Jun 3 – 24 | **The Deep Bell** (bell 6) | Copperhook tinkers build a diving bell; the Glass Sea abyss. Boss Fri Jun 21: *the Abyssal Shepherd*. Midsummer weekend. L5 Jun 24 — then the Choir burns Hollowmere and steals the Marsh Bell. |
| Jul 1 – 29 | **The Hush Abbey** | Pursuit, Greyholm isle, infiltration and siege. Boss Fri Jul 19: *Abbess Morrow Vane* (she escapes). The seventh bell is "the Tide's Tongue", inside the Underreef. The Lighthouse Concord. |
| Aug 5 – 26 | **The Fogwyrm** | L6. Concord council; hunt the young Fogwyrm in the sea-stacks, boss Fri Aug 16; its hoard holds the Underreef gate-map. Companion: Oaken (owl). The descent begins. |
| Sep 2 – 30 | **The Underreef** | Drowned coral halls. The Tide's Tongue is cracked. Boss Fri Sep 13: *the Bell-Breaker*. L7. Escape; journey to Emberwell's bellfounder. |
| Oct 7 – 28 | **The Recasting** | Star-iron from the Cinder Moors, the casting, Choir sabotage, harvest festival. L8. Thu Oct 31: the Night of Drowned Lights — *the Drowned Host*. |
| Nov 4 – 25 | **The Long Fog** | Fog covers the Marches; the Pale Herald returns as *the Pale Heir*. Defend the Concord lighthouses; gather a ringer for every bell. Boss Fri Nov 29: *Morrow Vane, the Hush Unbound* (a redemption choice). |
| Dec 2 – 22 | **The Longest Night** | Ringers to their towers; the hero carries the Tide's Tongue to the Underreef throat. Thu Dec 19: *the Pale Heir*. **Boss weekend** Dec 21–22: *the Pale Tide*. |
| Dec 23 – 31 | **Lamp Night** | Dawn, the Lamp Feast (Dec 25), rest, and New Year's Eve on the tower. The campaign ends on Dec 31. |

New content (all in `content-year.ts`): enemies scaled month by month, gear
for every slot over the year, stronger healing and combat consumables, four
companions, and level 3–8 specs for all six wardens (new abilities at 4, 6, 8).
