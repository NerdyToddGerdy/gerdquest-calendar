# Saving Santa — the December advent campaign

A short **seasonal** campaign: 25 pages, one "door" a day from December 1 to
Christmas morning. It can only be started in December, every run begins on
December 1 of the current year, and it comes back every year (each December
is a new run; earlier runs stay on the desk and stay playable). The engine
side is `season` on the catalog entry (`engine/types.ts`) and the seasonal
helpers in `engine/campaign.ts`.

Santa, sleighs, reindeer and the North are public folklore. **Every named
character, place, creature and plot beat here is original to GerdQuest
Calendar**: Pinecrook, the Hush, hushlings, Sixpence, Tansy Brightwick, the
Bell of First Snow, the Star-Lantern and the Silent Palace.

## The story

Santa's sleigh comes down empty in the snowwood outside Pinecrook. The Hush, a
winter spirit with no mouth who wants the world silent forever, has taken him
north beyond the aurora. The hero follows: rescues a lost reindeer, wakes the
silent workshop and its folk, rings the ice bell, finds the Star-Lantern, and
flies over the lights to the Silent Palace on Christmas Eve.

| Doors | Beat | Boss |
|---|---|---|
| 1–7 | The empty sleigh, the torn list, Sixpence on the ice, the cocoa house | Gingerbread Brute (7) |
| 8–14 | The north road, bells under the ice, the silent workshop and its folk | Nutcracker General (14) |
| 15–21 | The Bell of First Snow, carollers in the dark, the coal mine, the Star-Lantern | Snow Wyrm, the longest night (21) |
| 22–25 | Over the lights, the Silent Palace, Christmas Eve, Christmas morning | The Hush (24) |

Rest pages on 6, 13 and 20 (shops); level-ups on 13 and 20 (level 3 cap).
Heroes are chosen at the start: Juniper Tock (Toymaker), Rowan Thistledown
(Reindeer Wrangler), Marigold Puddingstone (Kitchen Witch), Nell Carroway
(Bellringer). Modern rules. No page text names a weekday, because the doors
land on different weekdays every year.

## Holiday rewards (earned by play only)

- Keepsakes at 3 / 7 / 12 / 18 / 25 days: paper snowflake, jingle bell (the
  weekly reroll), glass icicle ornament, Sixpence's harness tag, the star from
  the top of the tree.
- Looks: Wrapping paper, Candy-cane dice, Tinsel edge. Tokens: Holly rim,
  Candy-stripe rim, Star-topper emblem.
- Achievements: **Santa saved** (finish), **Every door on its day** (play all
  of December 1–25), **On the Nice List** (finish with 6+ virtue), **Back next
  December** (runs in two different Decembers).
