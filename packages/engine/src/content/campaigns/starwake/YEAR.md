# The Starwake — the voyage (the full year, Jan 1 – Dec 31, 2022 format)

A sea campaign with one chapter per month, twelve in all (313 pages). Months
1–9 carry the hunt for the shards; Months 10–12 take the voyage over the Rim
and home. Its structure through September follows the user's nine-chapter *Voyage of the Fallen Star* box set:
a port opening, an island hunt for star-shards, a climb, a strange island, a
deathly sea, the star's resting place, a forge, a library and a final battle.
**Every name, character, place and plot beat here is original to Questender.**
None of the box set's story, NPCs, places or monsters are reused.

## The premise

For a thousand years, sailors on the **Saltreach** have steered by the **Night
Chart**: the fixed stars that a keeper-star named **Wend** holds in place. Last
autumn Wend fell. Sages watched it break apart over the western sea. Since
then the stars have been drifting. Compasses spin at dusk, ships miss
their harbors, and whole fishing fleets have vanished into open water.

Wend isn't dead. Its **heart** sank somewhere far out, and five **shards** of
its light scattered across the islands. Gather the shards and they will point
to the heart. Rekindle the heart and carry it to the **Rim**, the edge of the
world where sea meets sky, and Wend can climb home. Then the stars stand still
again.

The rival is **Ashe Calloway, the Corsair-Prince**. He is a charming,
ruthless privateer whose fleet, the **Gull's Teeth**, rules the eastern
lanes. Ashe doesn't want Wend to go home. He wants to cage the heart in his
flagship's lantern, so that his ships, and only his ships, can find their
way across a sea where everyone else is lost. Ashe has his own shard-seeker,
the **Hollow Needle**, cut from the same falling star. Wherever the hero is
headed, his sails are never far behind.

## The cast

- **The hero** is a deckhand left stranded when the drifting stars wrecked
  their ship.
- **Greta Holloway** is a retired chart-maker with one good eye and a sloop
  called the *Second Chance*. She hires the hero and becomes the voyage's
  guide.
- **Vessa Quill** is a diver-scholar who first works for Ashe and then
  switches sides (Month 5).
- **Ashe Calloway** commands from the *Gilt Gull*. He is the rival in every
  month. He escapes at the end of Month 9, and the final showdown is saved
  for the later chapters.
- **Bosun Harrow Teague** is Ashe's enforcer, a recurring brute who is
  finally beaten in Month 7.

## Pacing

The pacing matches the other campaigns: Mon–Fri pages plus a combined Sat/Sun
page. Most weekends are **ports** with Rest, shopping and sometimes a crew
member to hire. Each month has one big boss, usually on its last Friday.
Level-ups land on port weekends, roughly one every month and a half (Lv 7 on
Oct 22–23, Lv 8 on Nov 26–27). The year's last Saturday, Dec 31, is a one-day
page. Bosses: one a month on or near the last Friday, and two in December.

## The arc

- **Months 1–2:** The drifting stars are a background disaster. Ashe is a
  rumor and a press-gang.
- **Months 3–4:** The shards start changing the places where they fell. The
  hero learns the star is *alive*, and Ashe's Needle shows up one shard
  behind every time.
- **Month 5:** The rivals are trapped together, and Vessa defects.
- **Months 6–7:** The hero finds the heart and wakes it.
- **Month 8:** The hero races Ashe for the chart.
- **Month 9:** The hero reaches the edge of the world, only to find the way
  home broken and Ashe gone ahead into the sky.
- **Month 10:** Over the Rim, the hero learns the stair, the keystone and
  what Ashe's family took.
- **Month 11:** The shards go home, the stair lights, and Ashe tries to cage
  Wend. He fails.
- **Month 12:** Ashe is beaten at the gate. At the longest night the Needle
  is filled with light instead of a star, Wend climbs home, and the Night
  Chart snaps back into place. Ashe survives and becomes a (bad) ferryman.

**Ending (Dec 31, a single-day page):** the first clear night in a year, with
every ship on the Saltreach finding its way home.

## Recurring threads

- **Wend's light.** Each shard gives the hero a small new trick, such as
  seeing in the dark, calming water or reading a lie in someone's eyes. At the
  end of the voyage, the hero has to give them all back.
- **Greta's past.** Greta drew the map that Ashe's father once used to raid
  her home port. She owes the sea a debt, and she tells the hero about it in
  Month 6.
- **The *Second Chance*.** The hero upgrades the sloop at ports: a new sail,
  a reinforced hull, the diving bell and storm-rigging. Its condition matters
  in the Month 7 storm, the Month 9 battle and the voyage beyond.
