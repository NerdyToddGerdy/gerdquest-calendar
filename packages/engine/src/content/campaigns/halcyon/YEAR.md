# The Halcyon Debt: the year outline

The campaign is original, written for 2023 (Jan 1 was a Sunday) and played on the frontier rules.

**Page format**
- Mon–Fri: one page each.
- Sat + Sun: one combined weekend page (`span: 2`).
- 313 pages in total: 25 in the January arc, then 48 weeks × 6 = 288.
- Each week is built with `week(month, mondayDay, [mon, tue, wed, thu, fri, weekend])`, and the dates are computed from it.
- Weeks are filed in the month their Monday falls in. The one exception is `pages/feb.ts`, which starts with the week of Mon Jan 30 because that week opens the February arc.

**Threads that run all year**
- **The Halcyon Debt.** The ship is mortgaged to the **Ledger Combine**, a creditor-cartel that runs the Shoal's banks. Its **Auditors** repossess ships, and sometimes people.
- **The data core** that Juniper carried off Tollgate Ring holds a survey of the **Choir**: a precursor relay network buried under the Shoal. Wake it and new jump lanes open that no toll gate can tax. The Combine wants it buried. The **Core Navy** (Commodore Ansel Rhee) wants it controlled. Free spacers want it open.
- **Captain Oriel Vell** (beaten in January) is left in Navy custody. He doesn't stay there.
- **Allies:** Juniper Okafor (now the ship's quartermaster) and Fenn's Shipyard (the free-yard).

**Cadence**
- **A typical week:** a story Monday, two check pages, a fight or dogfight, a Friday payoff, then a weekend.
- **Weekends:** three of four each month are Rests, with a shop and/or dock. One is an action weekend (dogfight or event, no Rest).
- **Bosses:** a ground boss roughly every month, plus a big dogfight most months.
- **Level-ups** (all on weekend pages): L3 Mar 11, L4 May 13, L5 Jul 15, L6 Sep 16, L7 Nov 11, L8 Dec 30.
- **Check difficulty rises during the year:** 11 in Feb, 12 in Mar–Apr, 13 in May–Jul, 14 in Aug–Oct, 15 in Nov–Dec.

| **Apr** | *Breakers* | The **Breaker Belt** ion storm (Malfunction hazards). Rescue mining town **Coppice**, besieged by Mother Sela Quin's storm raiders. | Sela's flagship *Squall* (dogfight), then Sela (Apr 28). | Crew Nadia Kovač, Ghost Drive. |
| **May** | *The Choir Wakes* | The core's second layer decodes. Commodore Rhee's Navy shows up, polite and armed. A race to the **Lacuna** relay node. | The Choir Sentinel (May 26). | Level 4 (May 13). Navy frigate in a dogfight. |
