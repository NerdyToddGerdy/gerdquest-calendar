# The Ember Road — the year (2022 format, classic rules)

The waystones that light the frozen north road are being snuffed by **the Hush**,
a silent cult that serves **the Long Dark**, an old winter that wants the world
quiet. Tamsin Reed (prologue) hands her lantern to the chosen hero at the
Cinderford muster; from then on the hero is a keeper of the road.

Pacing (every month): Mon–Fri pages + a combined Sat/Sun page. Most weekends
are havens (Rest + shop, sometimes followers for hire). One bigger boss a month,
usually on a Friday, fought solo under the Stun rule. Level-ups land on haven
weekends roughly every seven weeks.

Escalation: Jan–Feb the Hush snuffs stones by night; Mar–Apr it changes the land
(thaw, moss); May–Jun it reaches into forges and courts; Jul–Aug the heroes chase
it to its abbey and lose Tamsin; Sep it marches openly; Oct–Nov the heroes go
under and over the road to its heart; Dec the Long Dark wakes and is put out.

All characters, places and plot are original to Questender.
