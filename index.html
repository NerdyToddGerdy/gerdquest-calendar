Open your desk

One page of adventure, every day

Questender is a solo role-playing game that unfolds on a calendar. Each real date has its own page. Read it, roll the dice, make the call, and tomorrow's page waits until tomorrow.

Start playing

Free, in your browser. No account needed.

How it works

  1. One page per day

    Every campaign is a year of calendar pages. A page unlocks on its real date, so there is no reading ahead and no binge to burn through. Come back tomorrow.

  2. Several campaigns at once

    Keep a lamp-lit fog story on the left of your desk and a deep-space chase on the right. Each has its own party, its own pack and its own calendar.

  3. Your saves stay with you

    Progress is stored in this browser on this device. Nothing is uploaded, and you can export a backup whenever you like.

  • The Halcyon Debt

    Space-frontier courier run

  • The Starwake

    Forest and city, legacy chapters
